How accurate is this?

Browsers cannot read the physical size of a screen, so the size is worked out from the screen you pick. This assumes the browser is on the laptop's built-in screen, in a window at 100 % zoom. On an external monitor or at another zoom level the sizes will be off. Use the bank card to correct it.

Nothing is stored and nothing leaves your browser. Your choices live in this page's link, so you can share it.
