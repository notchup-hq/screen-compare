How accurate is this?

Browsers cannot read the physical size of a screen, so the size is worked out from the screen you pick. This assumes the browser is on the laptop's built-in screen, in a window at 100 % zoom. On an external monitor or at another zoom level the sizes will be off. Use Fine-tune with a bank card to correct it.

If the link does not name your screen, the page guesses it from the screen's resolution and offers it as a suggestion. Display scaling, browser zoom and external monitors change what the browser reports, so check the guess. It runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.

Nothing is stored and nothing leaves your browser. Your choices live in this page's link, so you can share it. Bookmark this page to keep this comparison; nothing else is saved.
